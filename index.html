About

I’m a Master’s student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Alberta, working where robotics, machine learning and human motor learning meet. In the IDEA Lab, under the supervision of Dr. Milad Nazarahari and Prof. Albert H. Vette, I design control strategies and data-driven algorithms that adapt robotic rehabilitation to each person in real time, keeping therapy challenging enough to drive learning, without becoming discouraging.

My path here started in electrical engineering at K. N. Toosi University of Technology, where I designed a digital blood pressure monitor and worked on oscillometric and PPG signal processing, from active filter circuits and PCBs to estimation algorithms. Alongside my studies, I spent seven years as an IT manager looking after a company’s systems, network and website.

Today my work spans reinforcement learning, deep learning, computer vision and biosignal processing. Two of our papers were published at IEEE BioRob 2026, and I also teach: this term I’m a teaching assistant and lecturer for Applied Computational Intelligence for Engineers.

Outside the lab, I volunteer at research and engineering conferences on campus. I speak Persian and English, and some French.
